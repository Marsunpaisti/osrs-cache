Most of this library is ported from RuneLite's cache library 1.13.5
(net.runelite:cache, https://github.com/runelite/runelite/tree/master/cache).
Paths below are under its net/runelite/cache/:

- src/store.cpp, src/buffer.h: fs/Container, fs/Archive, fs/Index,
  fs/ArchiveFiles, fs/jagex/DiskStorage, fs/jagex/IndexFile,
  fs/jagex/DataFile, fs/flat/FlatStorage, index/IndexData, io/InputStream,
  util/Xtea, util/Djb2
- src/definitions.cpp: definitions/loaders NpcLoader, ItemLoader,
  ObjectLoader, SequenceLoader, KitLoader, SpotAnimLoader, UnderlayLoader,
  OverlayLoader, HitSplatLoader, HealthBarLoader and EntityOpsLoader, their
  definitions, EntityOpsDefinition and ItemManager
- src/configs.cpp: definitions/loaders EnumLoader, ParamLoader,
  VarbitLoader, StructLoader, InventoryLoader, AreaLoader, DBRowLoader,
  DBTableLoader, DBTableIndexLoader and GameValLoader, their definitions,
  DBTableIndexManager, util/ScriptVarType and util/BaseVarType
- src/interfaces.cpp, src/scripts.cpp, src/disassembler.cpp:
  definitions/loaders InterfaceLoader and ScriptLoader, their definitions,
  definitions/ClientScript1Instruction, InterfaceManager,
  script/disassembler/Disassembler, script/Instructions, script/Opcodes,
  script/RuneLiteInstructions and script/RuneLiteOpcodes
- src/map.cpp: definitions/loaders MapLoader and LocationsLoader, their
  definitions, region/RegionLoader, region/Region, region/Location,
  region/Position, region/HeightCalc and util/KeyProvider
- src/model.cpp, src/frames.cpp: definitions/loaders ModelLoader,
  FrameLoader and FramemapLoader, their definitions (ModelDefinition's
  computeNormals, computeTextureUVCoordinates and computeAnimationTables),
  models/VertexNormal and models/FaceNormal
- src/sprites.cpp: definitions/loaders SpriteLoader, FontLoader and
  TextureLoader, and their definitions (SpriteDefinition.normalize,
  TextureDefinition.method2680)
- src/worldmap.cpp, src/audio.cpp: definitions/loaders WorldMapLoader,
  WorldMapCompositeLoader, WorldMapDataLoader, WorldMapElementLoader,
  TrackLoader and sound/SoundEffectLoader with SoundEffect1Loader to
  SoundEffect3Loader, their definitions, WorldMapManager, io/OutputStream
  and region/Position
